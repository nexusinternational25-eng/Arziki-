How it works

  1. You submit your firm order, the supplier's quote and the guarantees you offer.
  2. ARZIKI checks your client, the supplier and the prices.
  3. A partner buys the goods and resells them to you on deferred terms, at a price fixed in writing.
  4. Contracts and guarantees are signed before a notary. The supplier is paid directly.
  5. You deliver your client and pay on the due date.

For SMEs and large companies alike, with a network of suppliers who also accept deferred payment. Togo, Niger, Benin.

Frequently asked questions

What is a funded order?

You have a firm order from an identified client. A partner buys the goods from the supplier and resells them to you with a fixed mark-up, payable on an agreed due date. You know the price before signing.

Does ARZIKI advance money?

ARZIKI does not lend money or collect savings. Every operation is governed by contracts signed before a notary. Any participation carries a risk of loss. ARZIKI is an intermediary: it checks, connects and earns a commission.

Which documents should I prepare?

The purchase order or contract, the supplier's quote, the company's trade register and tax numbers, the manager's ID and the beneficial owner declaration.
