Our expertise

Funding, business, legal and training

All our services, grouped into four areas, for a single contact from the first meeting to project success.

The four areas

Financing

  • Resource mobilisation and fundraising
  • Financial engineering and funding applications
  • Investment memorandums and financial models
  • Public-private partnerships (PPP)
  • Real estate projects: feasibility, funding, structuring

Business and brokerage

  • Deal brokerage and origination
  • Institutional advocacy
  • Market studies and market entry
  • Commercial, financial and operational due diligence
  • Support to obtain approvals and licences

Legal and debt recovery

  • Amicable international debt recovery, with our partner firm AMA
  • Complex and sensitive cross-border cases
  • Legal and contract support (OHADA, WAEMU)
  • Business agent and introductions

Training

  • Tailor-made training: on site, remote, in-house
  • Governance, financial management, leadership
  • Humanitarian law, child rights, data protection
  • Youth entrepreneurship

Our services in detail

Resource mobilisation and fundraising

We identify suitable funding sources (banks, funds, donors, investors), prepare the file and support the meetings until the decision.

Financial engineering and investment memorandums

Deal structuring, financial model, investment memorandum: clear, quantified and verifiable documents.

Public-private partnerships and real estate projects

Support for complex PPPs and real estate projects: feasibility studies, funding search, legal and contractual structuring.

Brokerage, deal origination and institutional advocacy

We connect companies with partners, clients and institutions, and present their cases to decision-makers.

Market studies, market entry, approvals and licences

Sector and feasibility studies, market entry roadmap, procedures to obtain approvals and licences without administrative delays.

Due diligence

Commercial, financial and operational analysis of an opportunity or a partner, for informed decisions.

Amicable international debt recovery

With our partner firm AMA: cross-border, complex and sensitive cases, handled discreetly. Average time 21 to 90 days. More than 15 years of experience.

Legal and contract support

Contracts, compliance and securing your operations under OHADA law and WAEMU rules.
